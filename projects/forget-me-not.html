TOOLS

FORMAT
Immersive light and video installation

Fitting moving image to a room.

Hypergraphia provided technical setup and specialized support for this immersive light and video installation by Victoria.

The work fitted the artist's video content to the complex surfaces of the exhibition space, including the bed, walls, and sink.

On-site programming managed calibration, playback, and the final light loop.
