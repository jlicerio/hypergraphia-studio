Triqueta
AR / VR

AR overlays and a VR experience built around Michael Flanagan's paintings and video works.

ROLE
Technical development / immersive media
TOOLS
Unreal, AR, VR, AI-assisted art
PARTNER
Michael Flanagan

Painting becomes a portal.

Triqueta was a collaborative exhibition project developed for the McAllen Creative Incubator.

Hypergraphia created a VR experience around Flanagan's video works and built AR overlays for original paintings.

The installation connected traditional painting, AI-generated elements, and new media in one spatial narrative.
